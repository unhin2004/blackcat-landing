Managing properties for other people?

Running a property management company means spending hours each month compiling owner statements in spreadsheets, tracking commission splits manually, and emailing PDFs to each owner separately. Property Management tier automates the whole stack: per-owner statements with commission + pass-through expenses, secure owner portal where each owner sees only their own properties (never yours, never other owners'), per-owner Schedule E reports at tax time, and team-member accounts so your staff can operate the dashboard without seeing company financials.

  • Owner statements with commission breakdown, expense pass-through, and pro-rata splits for shared utilities on multi-unit properties

  • Everything in Business tier (unlimited properties, API, white-label reports, advanced cleaner ops)
