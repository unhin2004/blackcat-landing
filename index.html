Plaid Bank connection

Your bank password never touches our servers.

Plaid handles the bank login — the same connection layer Venmo, Robinhood, and your bank's own mobile app use. We only receive the transaction data Plaid shares back. Disconnect any bank in one click from Settings, which immediately revokes our access.

Stripe Payments

We don't see or store your card number.

Subscriptions are billed through Stripe. We receive a subscription status and the last four digits of your card — nothing more. Manage or cancel any time through your Stripe customer portal (linked from Settings), no support email required.

Your data

Yours to export. Yours to delete. Yours, full stop.

Export every transaction to CSV from Tax & Reports. Delete your entire account from Settings → Danger Zone — no support ticket, no waiting. We don't sell your data, we don't share it for advertising, and AI features only process your data when you ask them to.
