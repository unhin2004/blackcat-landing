How This Calculator Works

Today: Airbnb deducts a 3% host fee from your payout, and your guest pays a ~14% service fee on top of your price at checkout. From September 15, 2026: one 15.5% fee comes out of your payout and the guest-side fee disappears — so an unchanged price is a ~12-point pay cut that also hands your guests a discount. The break-even price increase is +14.8% (your payout ÷ 0.845), not the +18.3% often shared online — that larger figure nets you your full current listed price, which is a raise. The full math, including what the change does to your 1099-K and Schedule E, is in our 15.5% host fee explainer.

One honest caveat: these numbers hold if your occupancy holds. Nobody knows exactly how demand shifts when every US listing's sticker price rises at once while checkout totals stay flat — watch your bookings for a few weeks after repricing and adjust with data, not vibes.
